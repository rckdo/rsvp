Palette

Authored in sRGB; convert to CMYK for litho printing.

Typography

Google Fonts: Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, Marcellus, Parisienne.

Quilt block library

Every block is a square grid of cells: solid fills or half-square triangles. Seven in use, four held in reserve.

Placement sequences

Hand-composed: no two adjacent blocks share a shape or a colour, no shape-and-colour pair repeats within a piece, and the two square-on-point blocks are never adjacent. The reply-card reverse is the one generated field.

Bleed 1.5 mm per edge (included above); text at least 5 mm inside trim; square corners everywhere; no border radius, no gradients beyond the quilt construction. Browser print-to-PDF could not fully embed the fonts, so the print run used rasterised PNGs at roughly 384 DPI; a vector rebuild with embedded fonts remains the better long-term route.
